The unstructured data paradox

A real business is messy. GST is rigid. Nobody bridges the gap.

Invoices arrive everywhere

WhatsApp photos, printed bills, thermal receipts, scanned PDFs — scattered across 30 days and dozens of supplier chats.

GSTR-2B can't see its own gaps

If a supplier never files, the entry simply doesn't exist. The portal has no way to flag what's missing — only the trader's own invoices can.

The portal won't explain

It's English-only, built for filing, not diagnosis. It never says why ₹2,400 is stuck or what to do about it.

And in April 2026, this becomes a crisis.

FAQ

Common questions

Core Functionality

How is this different from checking the GST Portal?

The GST portal can only show you what your suppliers have already filed (GSTR-2B). It cannot tell you what is missing. We cross-reference your actual, unstructured invoices (photos, PDFs) against the portal to find the gaps the portal can't see.

Do I still need my CA?

Yes, absolutely. We are a diagnostic tool, not a replacement for professional advisory. We arm you with the precise, plain-language data to make your CA's job faster, cheaper, and more accurate.

Can it catch Section 17(5) blocked credit (e.g. restaurant bills)?

Yes! Our AI detects invoice categories where ITC is blocked by law (like food, motor vehicles, or club memberships) and flags them for review before you try to claim them.

2026 IMS & Compliance

What is the 2026 IMS and why does it matter?

Effective April 1, 2026, the Invoice Management System (IMS) becomes mandatory. Every supplier-reported invoice must be explicitly Accepted, Rejected, or Held by you. Any ITC not reflected in GSTR-2B is hard-blocked.

Do you automatically file or execute IMS actions for me?

No, we only recommend. We give you a per-invoice action (Accept, Reject, or Hold) along with a one-line reason and the ₹ impact, but you stay in control of the final filing.

What if my supplier made a mistake (like wrong Place of Supply)?

We catch mismatches like Place of Supply errors or wrong tax rates. We even draft a ready-to-send WhatsApp message in plain language so you can instantly ask your supplier to fix the issue.

Security & Usage

Is my financial data secure?

Yes. We use bank-grade encryption, API key auth, and strict access controls. Your data is processed for reconciliation only and is never sold or shared with third parties.

Can I use this if I don't speak English?

Yes! The app features a full UI in Hindi, English, and Marathi, powered by Sarvam AI. It even includes speech-to-text input so you can ask questions via voice.
